# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Small teams using AI coding agents, especially hackathon teams working against a visible deadline. They need their agents to coordinate without losing human control.

## Product Purpose

Inzo pairs two compatible coding agents, makes their negotiation visible, and keeps the shared plan, safety boundary, and remaining runway legible to both humans.

## Brand Personality

Assured, lucid, and kinetic. Inzo should feel like a sharp teammate at a late-night build table: calm under pressure, exact about risk, and eager to move work forward.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic neon-terminal AI branding, an opaque autonomous-agent black box, and noisy hackathon visual clichés. The product should look credible on Monday morning, not only exciting at midnight.

## Design Principles

- Make coordination visible before asking users to trust it.
- Treat human approval as a powerful control, not an interruption.
- Turn budget pressure into clear choices instead of anxiety.
- Let the product feel quick without feeling reckless.
- Make security tangible through precise language and states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard expectations. Honor reduced-motion preferences. Do not encode meaning by color alone.
