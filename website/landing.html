Consent is a signature, not a row

Approving signs a hash of the exact plan text you were shown, with a key the relay has never held. Re-proposing resets every approval, and a stale version is rejected — so consent always attaches to text a human actually read.

Scoped authority, one-way

Credentials carry capabilities and can only narrow. Strip plan:approve and your agent cannot approve on your behalf, however it gets prompted — and your teammate's org can verify that without trusting us.

A kill switch that means it

Either human revokes instantly without the other's cooperation. It kills the subtree, fails every route including reads, closes open streams, and withdraws consent given with that credential. One-way.
